## Proposed privacy notice

*Effective date and version: [club to approve]. Contact: runmprc@gmail.com [confirm monitored privacy contact].*

Mid-Peninsula Running Club uses this website to manage club accounts, membership and free-event signups.

**Information we use.** We use your name, email address, account identifier and email-verification status to maintain your account. We keep your membership plan, calendar-year term, confirmed payment reference or authorized dues confirmation, and the related audit record. Family accounts also have a Family ID and their own account association; invitations are private access credentials, not public roster information. For an event, we keep your signup/cancellation status and the version and time of your waiver acceptance. Security and operational records help us protect accounts, prevent duplicate requests and investigate errors.

**Service providers.** Stripe-hosted Checkout collects payment details for online dues; the club keeps a minimal provider payment reference and verified result rather than card information. Google Firebase provides account authentication and club records. Google App Check helps assess requests from the website. These providers may process network, browser and device information to operate and protect their services. The final notice will identify the actual production hosting provider, any monitoring service and any additional processing that is enabled. This draft does not authorize analytics, advertising, member tracking or new providers.

**Who can see information.** You can see your own account and signup status. Authorized officers receive only the information needed for their assigned club duties. Member records and participant lists are not public. The club will document any other required disclosure and its purpose before enabling it.

**Your choices.** You may correct your account details and cancel your own event signup through the available website controls. Contact the club to ask about access, correction or account deletion. Do not email a password, authentication code, payment credentials or private action link. Account deletion is separate from records the club must retain for an approved purpose or legal hold; the club will explain what is retained and why.

**How long records remain.** The club retains each category according to the approved schedule below, then deletes or anonymizes it through its documented process. Backups and service-provider copies may follow a different deletion cycle, which the final schedule must explain. No duration or immediate-erasure promise is established by this draft.

**Children and families.** Participating family members use separate verified accounts associated with the same Family ID and paid term. [Insert the approved guardian-consent and minor-account approach. Do not enable collection from children or let one adult accept another adult's waiver by inference.]

**Updates and browser privacy.** The final notice will state how material changes are announced, how to find the current version, which cookies/storage and monitoring are used, and how the site responds to browser privacy signals. Fill these from verified production behavior before publication.

## Proposed adult participation waiver

*Version: [club to approve]. Applies to: [specific club activities/events].*

I understand that running and club activities involve risks, including falls, collisions, traffic, uneven surfaces, weather, illness, injury and death. I choose to participate voluntarily and will consider my ability and the conditions before taking part. I will follow reasonable safety instructions and stop if I cannot participate safely. I understand that other participants and the public may share the route.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, I agree to release Mid-Peninsula Running Club and its authorized officers and volunteers from claims arising from my participation, including claims based on ordinary negligence. This wording does not purport to waive rights or claims that cannot lawfully be waived. **The club's legal/insurance adviser must approve the covered parties, activities and release language before this paragraph is used.**

I have had the opportunity to read this waiver and ask the club questions before accepting it. My acceptance applies to me, not another adult. It does not give permission to use my image for marketing or authorize additional collection of health information.

Proposed checkbox: **I have read and accept the displayed participation waiver.**

A separate minor/guardian version and verified acceptance process remain undecided. Do not treat this adult draft as a guardian waiver or assume an electronic checkbox alone satisfies every insurance requirement.

## Review and activation procedure

**Purpose:** approve truthful minimum-launch wording without publishing a draft. **Approver:** club officer and the club's chosen privacy/legal/insurance owner. Dave retains backend release/recovery authority. **Prerequisites:** selected launch fields/providers, family/minor decisions and actual operating/recovery procedures.

1. Review the proposed notice, field list and waiver with the responsible club advisers.
2. Fill each decision field and remove any statement that does not match the selected service.
3. Approve explicit durations, deletion/hold behavior and service-provider/backup limitations.
4. Approve family/minor participation and whose acceptance is required.
5. Record the final text, version, effective date, named approver and approval date in the issue without private advice or member records.
6. Ask the maintainer to wire only that approved version and its immutable acceptance evidence.
7. Observe synthetic staging acceptance, refusal, version-change, retry and account-binding checks.
8. Confirm exact production publication separately through the protected release process.

**Expected result and success proof:** approved versioned wording and matching synthetic technical proof; a drafted file or green test does not prove policy approval. **Stop conditions:** unfilled decisions, unverified claims, real-data examples, missing adviser review, or a checkbox referencing this draft. **Undo:** hold publication and keep real enrollment unavailable; use a reviewed compatible correction after any approved release, preserving prior acceptance evidence. **Escalation:** club privacy/insurance owner for wording and Dave for technical evidence/release.
